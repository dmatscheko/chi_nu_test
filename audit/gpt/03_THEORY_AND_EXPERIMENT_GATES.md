| Lane | Current decision | Re-entry condition |

| Breadboard/single-channel electronics | **GO** if useful and inexpensive | Conventional electrical safety and calibration plan |

## 2. Theory work required first

### T1 — Select and realize the physical carrier

Supply a source law/receipt that:

- maps the actual device's state, ports, records, and refinement behavior to
  the selected finite scalar carrier;
- distinguishes a physical carrier attachment from a merely abstract
  A1–A3/repair architecture;
- remains identifiable against presentation/refinement alternatives relevant
  to that carrier.

**Pass artifact:** a source-bound construction and countermodel audit, not a
shared definition that makes the bridge true by `rfl`.

The separate global-capacity non-identifiability theorem concerns
\(D,N_{\rm in},M_0,N_{\rm out}\). It becomes a gate here only if a future
source/force derivation supplies a typed dependency on that closure.
Likewise, the Lean central-interface collar no-gos concern the Einstein branch,
not the protected-reserve χν coefficient, and are not a force gate without a
typed dependency.

### T2 — Resolve reserve semantics and freeze χν

The latest paper already makes the presence branch canonical as mathematics.
The experiment still has to freeze which operational reading and \(P\)
provenance its physical source consumes. Distinguish:

- projection-trace presence probability;
- mean occupancy count;
- finite sub-slot refinement;
- any Poisson limit.

Formalize the dictionary and recompute the exact coefficient/band. State
whether the CODATA-derived comparison pixel or a source-only \(P\) is being
used.

**Pass artifact:** theorem, provenance manifest, and one frozen coefficient
interval; if regulator independence or a continuum/Poisson limit is claimed,
also a finite-regulator/refinement-consistency receipt.

### T3 — Define an instrument-independent physical source

Derive a map from raw port records to a spatial
\(S_{\rm coh}^{\rm can}(x,t)\) with:

- units/normalization and uncertainty;
- quotient/presentation invariance;
- held-out predictive semantics;
- a registered mismatch-reduction/repair operation;
- saturation and zero rules;
- top, bottom, sum, difference, and volume integral;
- dummy, detuned, record-shuffled, and carrier-alternative controls.

It must be calibrated without balance data.

**Pass artifact:** frozen estimator, reference inputs, independent verifier,
and source-only hardware evidence bundle.

### T4 — Derive the repair-charge action

Either derive the rotor action from finite OPH repair dynamics or explicitly
declare it as a separately testable EFT hypothesis. Supply:

- compact canonical pair;
- globally single-valued source coupling;
- stable/positive constitutive law;
- causal/relativistic completion or a controlled nonrelativistic domain;
- source and boundary conditions.

**Pass artifact:** action, scope, well-posedness/stability analysis, and formal
conditional consequences.

### T5 — Calibrate the dimensional coupling \(q_\star\)

Derive or independently measure a nonzero \(q_\star\), with SI dimensions,
uncertainty, material/device dependence, and a protocol that does not infer it
from the force under test.

**Pass artifact:** independent calibration receipt and null sensitivity.

### T6 — Establish charge topology or outgoing momentum

Show how the device creates:

- nonzero integrated repair charge with its required field tail; or
- a measured outgoing repair-current/field-momentum flux.

A top-minus-bottom source difference is insufficient.

**Pass artifact:** charge/flux conservation ledger over a closed boundary.

### T7 — Generate and measure the external repair field

Specify:

- the field source;
- detector and calibration;
- boundary conditions and spatial profile;
- units and relation, if any, to ordinary gravity;
- an independently controlled reversal/rotation.

**Pass artifact:** field-only measurement with blinded controls. Do not assert
\(\nabla\theta_{\rm ext}=g\).

### T8 — Derive the sign operation

Derive either:

- a signed source orientation factor that reverses device charge; or
- force reversal under the independently measured reversal of
  \(\nabla\theta_{\rm ext}\).

Swapping two nonnegative source profiles is not enough.

**Pass artifact:** source/field sign law fixed before balance data.

### T9 — Close momentum and energy

Solve the actual device-plus-field boundary problem and account for:

- matter force;
- field momentum and stress flux;
- source creation/maintenance;
- toggle energy;
- radiation or boundary current;
- complete-cycle work.

**Pass artifact:** conserved stress-energy/current ledger with the experimental
boundary explicitly represented.

### T10 — Establish experimental identifiability

Show that pre-run source, charge, field, and calibration receipts imply a
prospective force distribution that alternatives cannot absorb. Freeze:

- magnitude interval and uncertainty;
- geometry/distance dependence;
- sign and phase;
- material dependence;
- null rule and affected claim scope;
- alternative models and discriminators.

**Pass artifact:** preregistration in which a null or detection has one
unambiguous claim-level consequence.

## 3. Hardware-source development while theory is open

The PCB work need not stop completely. It should be reorganized as a source
instrument with its own staged gates:

### H0 — Electrical bring-up

- correct pinouts, decoupling, footprints, and return paths;
- validate one channel;
- record complex impedance, \(f_s\), \(f_p\), \(Q\), ringdown, and calibration;
- generate ERC/DRC and manufacturing artifacts.

### H1 — Atomic records

- raw waveform or coherent I/Q plus amplitude;
- local append-only records;
- run/control IDs, hashes, clock, drive V/I/power, and environmental sensors;
- exact replay.

### H2 — Observer-like loop

```text
drive -> self-read -> record -> held-out prediction
      -> measured mismatch -> repair/update -> checkpoint -> repeat
```

Predeclare the mismatch, update, prediction loss, checkpoint tolerance, and
record-shuffled controls.

### H3 — Operational source-candidate article

- twelve physical boundary ports, or a source-bound construction showing how
  the measured ports realize a valid logical carrier/subfederation;
- physically separated zones;
- top/bottom estimators plus sum and volume integral;
- randomized active, detuned, dummy, shuffled-history, and power-matched sham;
- matched twin;
- source-only evidence bundle and independent witness.

Passing H3 would demonstrate a well-characterized observer-like metrology
article and operational source candidate. It would establish the current OPH
\(S_{\rm coh}^{\rm can}\) only after the independent T3 source map and
verifier also pass. It would not yet demonstrate repair charge or force.

## 4. Force-entry gate F0

A balance protocol may be called a χν/rotor force experiment only if **all**
items are independently frozen before force data:

- [ ] T1 physical scalar-carrier attachment
- [ ] T2 coefficient semantics and provenance
- [ ] T3 source estimator and source-only receipt
- [ ] T4 action/domain
- [ ] T5 \(q_\star\)
- [ ] T6 charge or outgoing flux
- [ ] T7 external repair field
- [ ] T8 sign law
- [ ] T9 conservation ledger
- [ ] T10 numerical prediction and identifiability
- [ ] H0–H3 instrument/source-candidate gates
- [ ] class-H raw capture, calibration, controls, custody, analysis binding,
      completeness, and independent attestation

If any item is absent, label the balance run a **theory-neutral
state-correlated-force search**, not an OPH or χν test.

## 5. Outcome scope after F0

Even after F0:

- a null can retire only the frozen coherent-matter/rotor force continuation in
  the tested domain;
- it does not erase finite consensus, screen algebra, or unrelated OPH
  branches;
- a positive result supports the frozen action only after ordinary mechanisms
  and rival new-force models are discriminated;
- independent replication or end-to-end independent witnessing remains
  mandatory for an extraordinary lift claim.
