## 1. Audit rule

The relevant inference is not “the hardware resembles an observer.” It is the
full chain

```text
finite OPH premises
  -> physically realized coherent source
  -> scalar-channel coefficient
  -> repair-charge dynamics
  -> dimensional device charge
  -> external repair field or outgoing momentum
  -> signed, conserved force
  -> calibrated numerical prediction
  -> identifiable experimental verdict
```

Every arrow must be independently supported. A theorem proving the consequence
of an assumed action does not derive the action. A simulator replaying a
declared finite structure does not establish a physical carrier. A balance
cannot be used to calibrate the same source, coupling, or field that the balance
is supposed to test.
